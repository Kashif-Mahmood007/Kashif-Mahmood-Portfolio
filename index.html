Research

Publications, datasets, and tools built as part of my research work.

A Deployment Risk Score Framework for Self-Supervised Chest X-Ray Classification: Calibrated Multi-Objective Evaluation Under Annotation Scarcity and Scanner Heterogeneity In revision
Kashif Mahmood, Romana Aziz, Muhammad Ramzan, Mahwish Ilyas, Ala Saleh Alluhaidan
Scientific Reports · 2026 · Nature Portfolio

This study asks which pretraining approach is actually safest to deploy for chest X-ray diagnosis when labeled data is scarce and scanners vary, rather than just which one scores highest.

  • Compared six (a contrastive and a reconstruction-based self-supervised models with five supervised baselines) on a 17,988-image, six-class chest X-ray benchmark across 10%, 20%, and 100% annotation budgets.
  • Achieved macro F1 = 0.952 with SimCLR + ResNet-50, statistically comparable to the fully supervised ceiling while retaining 88.8% of full-label performance at only 10% annotation; MAE + ViT-S/16 showed the strongest robustness to synthetic perturbations and the lowest overconfidence under calibration testing
  • Introduced a Deployment Risk Score (DRS) framework translating these accuracy, robustness, and calibration trade-offs into scenario-specific model-selection guidance for clinical deployment.
Lead authorIn Collaboration with the Authors of Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University
Deep Learning-Based Disease Detection in Peanut Cultivars Utilizing Transfer Learning Under review
Kashif Mahmood, Qaisar Abbas, Ume Habiba, Mahwish Ilyas, Turki Alghamdi, Hani Almoamari, Muhammad Ramzan
Scientific Reports · 2026 · Nature Portfolio

After EfficientNet and ConvNeXt variants fell short of state-of-the-art, I built a ResNet-50 transfer-learning approach with augmentation, a OneCycleLR schedule, and weighted loss to handle class imbalance.

  • Achieved 97.28% accuracy on a 1,720-image benchmark of healthy and diseased groundnut leaf images, surpassing the previous best published result of 96.51% on the same dataset.
  • Established a new baseline for automated groundnut leaf disease detection, demonstrating the feasibility of AI-based tools for sustainable agricultural practice.
Lead authorIn Collaboration with the Authors of Islamic University of Madinah
A Systematic Review of Visual Computing Technologies for Intelligent Peanut Agriculture Under review
Ume Habiba, Nabeel Abbas, Kashif Mahmood, Mahwish Ilyas, Muhammad Summair Raza, Muhammad Ramzan
USJICT · 2026 · University of Sindh, Jamshoro

A review of deep learning, machine learning, and hybrid approaches across four core tasks in peanut agriculture: variety classification, maturity assessment, disease detection, and pest recognition.

PakGroundnut-4V: A Multi-Condition Image Dataset for Pakistani Groundnut (Arachis hypogaea L.) Variety Classification Published

A large-scale, multi-condition image and phenotypic dataset built to support robust computer vision research in agricultural variety classification. Designed and collected independently, with images gathered across two sites in Punjab, Pakistan: Chakwal and Sargodha.

Varieties Attock-2019 (2,240 images), Fakhr-e-Chakwal (2,288), Parachinaar (2,192), Fakhr-e-Attock (2,224); verified through peer-reviewed literature and direct consultation with the Barani Agricultural Research Institute (BARI), Chakwal
Scale 8,944 images (320×320 px), fixed 90° overhead angle, across 5 backgrounds, 4 lighting conditions, and 5 quantity levels (1 to 5 peanuts per frame)
Negative class 60 background-only images, kept separate from the classification folders to support out-of-distribution and future object-detection research
Morphometric samples 80 pod photographs and measurements (20 per variety), taken with a digital Vernier caliper (0.01mm precision) and digital scale (0.1g precision)
Splits Standard stratified split (6,259 / 1,340 / 1,345 train/val/test), plus dedicated cross-background and cross-lighting splits for domain-generalization research
Baseline ViT-B/16 reaches macro-F1 of 0.9822 on the standard test split
Documentation README, LICENSE, CITATION.cff, and DATA_CARD.md, with full per-image metadata
License CC BY 4.0
Access Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22014996), Kaggle, GitHub
Authors Kashif Mahmood, Dr. Muhammad Ramzan, Khizar Hayat (farm owner who supplied physical specimens)
